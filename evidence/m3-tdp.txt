# M3 — TDP and power profiles for the ROG Ally (card t_88a807c2)

## What shipped

- `configs/rootfs/usr/local/bin/emunix-tdp` — three named profiles over `asusctl`
  (`profile set --ac/--battery`, so asusd follows the power source) plus `ryzenadj` for the
  package-power ceiling in mW. With no argument it applies the remembered profile (default:
  balanced on AC, quiet on battery); with a name it applies and remembers that profile in
  `/var/lib/emunix/tdp-profile`. Only STAPM/PPT limits are written — no Tctl or
  skin-temperature argument appears anywhere in the script.
- `configs/rootfs/etc/systemd/system/emunix-tdp.service` — applies the profile at boot,
  ordered after dbus. asusd is D-Bus activated: the Arch unit has no `[Install]` section,
  so enabling it would be a silent no-op.
- `configs/rootfs/etc/sudoers.d/30-emunix-tdp` — retrogamer may run exactly the three switch
  commands without a password, nothing else (`visudo -c`: parsed OK).
- `configs/rootfs/usr/share/emunix/power-profiles/{quiet,balanced,performance}.sh` plus a
  `<name>power</name>` system in `es_systems.xml`: firstboot seeds them into
  `/storage/roms/power` and ES-DE lists them under "Power Profiles".
- `profiles/handheld-common.txt`: dropped `power-profiles-daemon` (it races asusd for the
  kernel's platform_profile interface) and added `lm_sensors` for thermal telemetry.
  `profiles/aur.txt`: pinned `ryzenadj` (AUR commit 3534651beaca51f5a9ebb5502167e9d003d3dcb0).

## Measured sustained package power (5-minute load per profile)

Hardware actually measured: AMD Ryzen 7 5800H (Cezanne), 16 threads, Ubuntu 26.04,
kernel 7.0.0-38-generic — NOT a ROG Ally. ryzenadj v0.19.0 built from the upstream tag,
SMU reached through the /dev/mem fallback (no ryzen_smu module). Battery-side values,
because this board exposes no /sys/class/power_supply and emunix-tdp falls back to them.

Procedure: `ryzenadj --stapm-limit=W --fast-limit=W --slow-limit=W` (exactly what
emunix-tdp runs), then 300 s of 12 spinning python processes, sampled every 10 s with
`ryzenadj --info`; 28 samples kept per profile (first two dropped). Defaults recorded
before (STAPM/PPT = 54 W) and restored afterwards, verified by re-reading the PM table.

| profile | ceiling | PPT fast avg (min–max) | PPT slow avg (min–max) | STAPM avg | Tctl max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| quiet | 8 W | 8.07 W (7.88–8.25) | 8.07 W (8.01–8.17) | 7.99 W | 59.9 C |
| balanced | 10 W | 10.07 W (9.94–10.40) | 10.00 W (9.99–10.00) | 8.73 W | 62.0 C |
| performance | 15 W | 15.08 W (14.89–15.25) | 15.00 W (14.98–15.00) | 11.41 W | 71.3 C |

Raw samples: evidence/m3-tdp-measure.log.

STAPM VALUE is a long-window average and was still converging when each 300 s window
closed (it carries the previous profile's history), so it lags; PPT FAST/SLOW are the
sustained ceilings and track the set limit within about 0.1 W.

## What this does not prove

- Anything about the ROG Ally itself: no asusctl/asusd behaviour (there is no ASUS WMI on
  the test host), no AC/battery switching, no reboot survival on hardware, no real battery
  drain, no embedded-controller rollback.
- On the QEMU guest (no SMU) `emunix-tdp.service` fails loudly at boot — expected; it does
  not block the session. On the Ally it should apply, and BP must confirm that.
- The ES-DE menu proof is scan-based (systems entry + ES-DE's own "Total game count: 4" +
  the screenshot showing the Power Profiles carousel), not a controller click-through of a
  switch command.
- The sudoers grant parses but was not exercised end-to-end from ES-DE.
- power-profiles-daemon absence is verified in the image; its conflict with asusd is quoted
  from the ArchWiki, not measured here.

   `sudo ryzenadj --dump-table`, confirm the chosen profile survives a reboot, and check
   that `asusctl profile set Quiet|Balanced|Performance` accepts those names (if it does

2. Approve the passwordless sudo grant for retrogamer (three exact commands) — it is a
   deliberate security-relevant exception, needed because ES-DE runs the menu entries as an
   unprivileged user while the SMU is root-only.
3. Battery-side ceilings are 8/10/15 W and AC-side 9/15/25 W; confirm they suit the device.
